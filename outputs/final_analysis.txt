Final Analysis

The project implemented a fake news detection framework using NLP, machine learning, stylometric analysis, and explainable AI techniques.

Key observations:

- Linear SVM achieved the highest internal classification performance.
- TF-IDF outperformed Bag of Words in most experiments.
- Stylometric features improved interpretability and behavioral analysis.
- SHAP explainability successfully identified influential semantic and stylistic features.
- Leakage-aware preprocessing prevented artificial performance inflation.
- External validation revealed significant domain-shift and generalization limitations.

The project demonstrated that high internal accuracy does not necessarily guarantee strong real-world robustness across unseen datasets.
