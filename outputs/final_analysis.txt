======================================================================

+--------------------------+---------------------------------------------+
| Observation              | Analysis                                    |
+--------------------------+---------------------------------------------+

| Dataset Shift            | Structural differences affected learning    |
| Temporal Variation       | Contemporary articles differed from ISOT    |
+--------------------------+---------------------------------------------+

Observation:
The model generalized poorly on unseen contemporary data,
highlighting the difficulty of cross-domain fake news
detection.

======================================================================
5. KEY LEARNINGS
======================================================================

- High internal accuracy does not guarantee strong
  real-world robustness.

- Leakage-aware preprocessing is important for preventing
  artificially inflated results.

- Explainable AI improves transparency and trustworthiness
  of machine learning predictions.

- Fake news detection systems are highly sensitive to
  dataset distribution and writing-style differences.

- Classical NLP models remain highly effective when
  combined with proper preprocessing and feature
  engineering.

======================================================================
6. FINAL CONCLUSION
======================================================================

The project successfully developed an explainable fake
news detection framework using NLP, stylometric analysis,
machine learning, and SHAP-based explainability.

Among all evaluated models, Linear SVM with TF-IDF
achieved the best internal performance. The inclusion of
stylometric features improved interpretability and
behavioral analysis, while SHAP visualizations provided
transparent reasoning behind prediction outcomes.

External validation revealed significant domain-shift and
generalization challenges, demonstrating that fake news
detection remains a difficult real-world problem despite
high benchmark accuracy.

Overall, the project evolved beyond a simple classification
task into a robustness-aware and explainable NLP system
for misinformation analysis.
