External Validation Summary

The trained model was evaluated on a contemporary external fake news dataset to assess cross-domain robustness.

Observation:
- The model showed substantial performance degradation during external validation.
- Prediction bias toward a single class was observed.
- Results indicated the presence of dataset shift and vocabulary mismatch.

This experiment highlighted the importance of:
- robust preprocessing,
- explainable AI,
- and realistic evaluation strategies in fake news detection systems.
